Base Boundary Metric & Integer Rationalization Floor

Geometric divisor Ω(B) = √(B + 1) collapsing trailing radical noise into integer scalars

Standard positional numeral systems carry geometric boundary distortion across interval steps $(B^{x-1}, B^x]$[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).
