Framework Overview & Core Architecture

By leveraging a Base-3 parameterization, the framework collapses standard irrational geometric distortion metrics into a rational integer scalar ($\Omega = 2$) and maps deterministic modulo-6 arithmetic channels ($\mathcal{C}_1 = 6k + 1$ and $\mathcal{C}_5 = 6k + 5$) onto a $180^\circ$ anti-parallel phase engine.
